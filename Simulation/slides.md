### **SLIDE 1 — FACILITY FACTORY**
Making infrastructure accountability independently verifiable.

- A new accountability model for safety-critical infrastructure.
- Trust should not be a prerequisite for transparency.

### **SLIDE 2 — THE PROBLEM: TRANSPARENCY DEPENDS ON TRUST**

- Opaque measurements: External stakeholders cannot independently establish whether critical measurements have been disclosed completely.
- Information asymmetry: Facilities control much of the information outsiders need to assess their transparency commitments.
- Limited accountability: Inspectors need reliable ways to establish what was promised, what was recorded, and whether access was obstructed.

### **Central question:**
What happens when the entity being monitored is also the entity controlling the evidence?

### **SLIDE 3 — INTRODUCING FACILITY FACTORY**

A platform allowing infrastructure operators to register facilities, select transparency commitments, and establish independently verifiable accountability records.

### **The lifecycle:**
1. Register a facility.
​2. Select one of five predefined contractual frameworks.
3. Sign and register the agreements.
4. Operate under predictable, predefined smart-contract rules.
5. Enable independent inspection and verification.

### **SLIDE 4 — FIVE CONTRACTS. FIVE TRANSPARENCY FRAMEWORKS.**

- Facilities choose which of the five predefined contractual frameworks to adopt.
- Each framework establishes specific transparency commitments.
- Agreements define disclosure obligations and inspector rights.
- Commitments cannot be arbitrarily rewritten.
- Leaving the platform follows the predefined termination process.
- Previously recorded evidence remains independently verifiable.

### **Proposed legal model:**
If the platform receives legal recognition, violations of contractual commitments could trigger legal proceedings before a competent physical court.

### **SLIDE 5 — DECENTRALIZED INSPECTOR SELECTION**

Who watches the facilities?

1. Facility staking:
   Each facility stakes up to three members in the DAO.

2. Monthly random selection:
   Five DAO members are randomly selected to form the monthly selection committee.

3. Inspector ranking:
   The committee ranks eligible inspectors using an ordered preference system.

4. Inspector appointment:
   The five highest-ranked inspectors are selected for the month's inspection cycle.

5. Monthly renewal:
   The selection process is repeated every month.

### **SLIDE 6 — INSPECTORS ARE ACCOUNTABLE TOO**

Inspection is a responsibility, not an unrestricted privilege.

Before inspecting a facility, each selected inspector must sign a facility-specific compliance agreement.

### **The agreement establishes:**
- Compliance with the applicable inspection framework.
- Expected professional conduct.
- Obligations towards the inspected facility.

### **Two-sided accountability:**
- Facilities commit to transparency.
- Inspectors commit to compliance and good demeanor.
- Both parties operate under documented obligations.

### **SLIDE 7 — ACCOUNTABILITY HAS CONSEQUENCES**

### **Facilities commit to:**
- Transparency regarding specified events, including key rotations.
- Not obstructing authorized inspectors.
- Respecting their signed contractual obligations.

### **The accountability process:**
1. A commitment is established.
​2. Relevant events and evidence are recorded.
3. Evidence can be independently inspected and verified.
4. Violations can be documented.
5. Under the proposed legal framework, violations may trigger physical legal proceedings.

The platform does not replace the courtroom.
It improves the verifiability of the evidence that can reach it.

### **SLIDE 8 — FROM TRUST-BASED TO VERIFIABLE INFRASTRUCTURE**

Why would facilities register?

- Stakeholders no longer need to rely exclusively on facilities' own assurances.
- Signed commitments can be independently inspected.
- Specific recorded events can be cryptographically verified.
- Inspection history becomes publicly accessible.
- Predictable smart-contract rules reduce discretionary changes.

### **The expected outcome:**
Greater stakeholder confidence, independent observation, and transparency-driven engagement.

Replace "Trust us" with "Verify it yourself."
